About the race

ONE ROAD.
FOUR WAYS TO RUN IT.

Project Stride is built for runners who train for the whole year and want one morning that actually tests it — a certified 21K for the veterans, a 10K for the steady climbers, a 5K for first-timers and families, and a Run–Bike–Run Duathlon for the ones who want a different kind of hurt.

Every category shares the same start village, the same hydration crew, and the same finish chute — so however far you're going, you cross the same line everyone else does.

Before the gun goes off

FAQ

Bib and kit pickup runs 4:00–5:00 AM at Stride Village on race morning. Bring your confirmation email and a valid ID — the name on your bib must match your registration.

Each category has its own cutoff (5K 1.5 hrs, 10K 2 hrs, 21K 3.5 hrs, Duathlon 3 hrs). Sweeper vehicles pick up runners past cutoff and bring them to the finish — your chip time still counts, but course support closes.

Yes — bring your own bike in roadworthy condition. A helmet is mandatory for the entire bike leg, no exceptions, and your bike must be racked in your assigned transition slot before the gun.

Six stations across the longer courses, spaced roughly every 3–4K, each stocked with water, electrolyte drink, and a medical volunteer on standby.

The race runs rain or shine. We only delay or shorten the course for lightning, flooding, or a marshal safety call — updates go out by email and at the start village.

All registrations are final — no refunds, no transfers, no deferrals to next year. Double-check your category, runner names, and shirt sizes before you confirm.

On the course

RACE RULES

  • 01

    Your bib must be visible and worn on the front for the entire race.

  • 02

    Helmets are mandatory for the full bike leg of the Duathlon — no exceptions.

  • 03

    Stay on the marked route and follow all marshal and traffic-management instructions.

  • 04

    Keep one ear free of headphones on any stretch that shares the road with traffic.

  • 05

    No bib transfers or bandit runners — every person on course must be wearing their own registered bib.

  • 06

    Runners with pre-existing conditions should be medically cleared before starting the 21K or Duathlon.

  • 07

    Please don't litter — drop cups and gels only at designated hydration stations.

  • 08

    Cutoff times are enforced. Sweeper vehicles will pick up runners past the cutoff for their category.

  • 09

    Organizers may withdraw any runner whose conduct is unsafe or disruptive, without refund.
