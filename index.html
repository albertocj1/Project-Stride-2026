About the race

LIMA ESTATE.
FOUR WAYS TO RACE.

Project Stride 2026 takes over the wide, open roads of Lima Estate in Malvar, Batangas. Whether you're running a brisk 3K fun run, pacing a steady 5K or 10K, or taking on the 5KM Run – 20KM Bike – 3KM Run Duathlon challenge, this event delivers a premier race experience.

All packages include a Race Shirt, Race Bib, Finisher’s Medal, and Bottled Water with hydration support throughout the course.
