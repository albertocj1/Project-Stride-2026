Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Project Stride ("we", "us", "our") organizes the Project Stride 2026 fun run and duathlon at Lima Estate, Malvar, Batangas on November 28, 2026. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you register through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We process personal information in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information we collect

When you register, we collect:

  • Contact details: full name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Emergency contact: the name and/or number of a person to contact if something happens to you on race day.
  • Race details: chosen category, shirt size, number of runners, names of additional runners in your group, and team name (if any).
  • Payment proof: the receipt or screenshot you upload for your GCash or bank transfer, and the amount due. We do not collect or store card numbers, bank passwords, or GCash PINs.
  • Race records: your registration status, assigned bib code, and check-in at kit pickup or on race day.

If you register other runners in your group, please make sure they agree to you sharing their names with us.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and confirm your registration and verify your payment.
  • To send you transactional emails: your registration confirmation with your bib code and QR code, and reminders to complete payment for an unpaid registration.
  • To prepare race kits, shirts, and bibs, and to check you in at kit pickup and on race day.
  • To contact you or your emergency contact about safety, schedule changes, or race-day incidents.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Email and Google services

We send registration emails through the Gmail API using our own organizer Gmail account. Our app only requests permission to send email from that account; it does not read, store, or access the contents of anyone's mailbox. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run the event:

  • Service providers that host our website and data: Supabase (database and receipt storage), Render (registration server), Vercel (website hosting), and Google (email delivery).
  • Event staff and marshals, who see the details needed for kit pickup, check-in, and race-day safety.
  • Medical or emergency responders, if needed to protect your health or safety.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

5. Photos and video

Photos and videos may be taken during the event and used on our social media and promotional materials. If you would prefer not to appear in published photos, email us and we will do our best to remove images where you can be identified.

6. How long we keep it

We keep registration information and payment receipts for up to one (1) year after the event for record-keeping, accounting, and dispute resolution, then delete or anonymize it. Unpaid registrations that expire may be deleted sooner.

7. How we protect it

Access to registration data is limited to authorized organizers through a password-protected admin dashboard. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored with our hosting providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct inaccurate data, to object to processing, to have your data erased or blocked, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below.

9. Children

Participants under 18 must be registered by, or with the consent of, a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for providing the minor's information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
